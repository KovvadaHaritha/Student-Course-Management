📊 Data Science

Explore data analysis, visualization, and machine learning basics.

Ask a Question of Your Data

Data analysis starts with a clear question. Collect relevant observations, check for missing or inconsistent values, and summarize what the data contains before drawing conclusions.

  • Define the question and the measurements you need
  • Clean and organize the collected data
  • Summarize patterns with counts and averages

Summarize and Visualize

A small table can be enough to practice grouping measurements and comparing results. Charts make trends easier to spot, but always label them clearly and keep the original context in mind. 

study_hours = [1, 2, 2, 3, 4]
average_hours = sum(study_hours) / len(study_hours)

print(f"Average study hours: {average_hours:.1f}")
  • Choose a chart that fits the question
  • Look for patterns without confusing correlation with cause
  • Use a train/test split when evaluating a basic model
