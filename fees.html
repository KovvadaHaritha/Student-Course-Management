Payment instructions

  1. Confirm the pending balance and due date above.
  2. Select a payment method shown as available. Only the clearly labelled demo option is configured here.
  3. Choose Pay Now (Demo) to record a simulated outcome. No card, bank, or UPI details are collected.
  4. Only a server-recorded successful demo result updates the paid balance and enables a receipt download.

Real payment methods remain unavailable until a gateway, server-side verification, and verified webhook are configured.

Payment troubleshooting

Payment failed

No amount is counted as paid unless the server records verified success. Check the history before retrying; if it still fails, report a payment issue.

Amount deducted but status is pending

This site does not process real payments. For a real provider, keep the provider reference and contact the administrator; do not retry until the payment is reconciled. A pending demo entry is not a bank charge.

Payment successful but receipt is unavailable

Refresh this page and check the recorded status. Receipts are available only for successful records. If the record is successful and the receipt is still missing, report the issue.

Duplicate payment attempt

Repeated requests with the same request key are idempotent. Check your history before trying again. Do not assume a bank reversal or refund unless it has been confirmed.

Payment deadline passed

Contact the administrator using Help & Support to confirm the due date and next steps. No late fee is charged by this demo.

How do I contact the administrator?

Submit a support request with category “Payment issue” and include the date and transaction reference. Never include a PIN, password, CVV or full card number.
