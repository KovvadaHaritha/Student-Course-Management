🎨 UI/UX Design

Practice designing clear, accessible, user-friendly experiences.

Start with the User

User experience (UX) design begins by understanding who needs the product and what they are trying to accomplish. A short interview or task observation can reveal needs that assumptions may miss.

  • Describe the audience and their goals
  • Map the steps a user takes to finish a task
  • Identify confusing or unnecessary steps

From Wireframe to Interface

A wireframe is a simple layout plan. Arrange content by importance first, then apply typography, color, and spacing to establish a consistent visual hierarchy.

  • Keep primary actions easy to find
  • Use consistent spacing and clear labels
  • Check color contrast and keyboard accessibility
  • Test a prototype with users and refine it
